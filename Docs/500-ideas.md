# Shrunk: 500 things to add

Grouped by what they do for a player: getting started, feeling good in the moment, having something to chase, and having a reason to come back tomorrow. Nothing here is built yet; things the game already has are left out. Numbers run straight through, so you can say "do 37, 112 and 340".

**Done so far:** 1 (first-run safety net), 4 (boss items: each boss leaves its own the first time, then any boss can), 9 (First Steps goals), 27 (pickups already pulled in), 31 (low-health heartbeat), 36 (close calls), 95 (item wall), 276 (mailbox letters), 352 (quick run), 160 (the rival Void), 221 (rescue cages), 247 (rule of the day), 291 (seasons), 461 (music for every world), 186 (the Kitchen Sink world, with floods), 3, 8, 10, 16, 19, 20, 25 (more help for new players), 26, 28, 32, 33, 37, 38, 42, 47, 50 (game feel), 57, 61, 64, 66, 70, 80 (combat), 86, 87, 99, 115, 117, 118 (synergies and items), 121, 124, 127, 134 (hero powers), 142, 155, 158 (sleeping and shiny enemies), 172, 173, 178, 184 (boss records, medals, quicker boss music), 236, 237, 238, 239, 251 (five new floor twists), 212, 222 (timed and flawless rooms), 263, 266, 267 (fish tank, lab cat, jukebox), 446, 452, 457 (warning colours, toggle fire, a can't-lose assist). Also from your ideas: the coded Vault with a raid-style trial.

**My top 20 picks** (biggest effect on new players staying, for the least work): 1, 4, 9, 27, 31, 36, 62, 95, 141, 160, 186, 221, 247, 276, 291, 320, 352, 371, 412, 461.

## Getting started (the first ten minutes)

1. A first run that can't really be lost: floor 1 enemies hit for nothing until the first item is picked up.
